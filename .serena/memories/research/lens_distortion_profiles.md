# Lens distortion profile research

- As of 2026-07-30, no public manufacturer/Cardboard viewer profile explicitly identifying the SHINECON SC-H12 was found. SHINECON's official QR page lists G01-G10 families, not SC-H12; do not silently substitute SC-G12 or another model.
- Official SHINECON QR source: https://www.shinecon.com/qr-code. Profiles decoded with Google's `cardboard_device.proto` include: G10/G05/G04 `K1=0.526, K2=-0.152`; G06 `0.396, -0.029`; G07 `0.101, 0.090`; G02 `0.150, 1.750`; G01 `0.150, 1.780`. This same-brand spread rules out a defensible universal coefficient for cheap phone viewers.
- Cardboard defines `p' = p * (1 + K1*r^2 + K2*r^4 + ...)`, mapping real-screen/output positions to virtual-screen/texture positions. `r` is in tan-angle units, not arbitrary normalized shader coordinates. Source: https://github.com/googlevr/cardboard/blob/master/proto/cardboard_device.proto#L95-L105.
- Google fallback/reference values are Cardboard v1 `K1=0.441, K2=0.156` and the official 2015/v2 specification `K1=0.33582564, K2=0.55348791`; they are references, not SC-H12 calibration. Sources: https://github.com/googlevr/cardboard/blob/master/sdk/device_params/android/java/com/google/cardboard/sdk/deviceparams/CardboardV1DeviceParams.java and https://gstatic.com/cardboard_assets/cardboard_manufacturers_kit.zip.
- If shader radius `r_n` is normalized and tan-angle radius is `r_tan = R*r_n`, the equivalent first-order coefficient is `k=K1*R^2`; an edge-matched first-order approximation of a two-term profile is `k_edge=K1*R^2+K2*R^4`. The shader must use per-eye lens-centered, physical-aspect-corrected coordinates.
- Accepted MVP decision (2026-07-30): expose one local `0..100%` strength, default `50%`, and map it to first-order `k = 0.004 * strength` (`k=0.20` by default, maximum `0.40`). `SbsRenderer` applies the output-to-source radial lookup in its existing GLES pass, measures radius in physical output pixels relative to half the shorter eye dimension, blacks out samples outside canonical UV, and bypasses fragment distortion at `0%`. This is a conservative fallback, not an SC-H12 calibration; validate and tune it with a straight-line grid on the OnePlus 11. No Cardboard SDK/profile ingestion was added.
